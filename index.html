How to Play GOT FIVE!

Goal: Be the first player to deduce all 5 of your own secret numbers in ascending order!

Setup: There are 60 unique tiles numbered 1 to 60 across 5 colors (Red, Blue, Green, Yellow, Purple), each bearing 1, 2, or 3 dots. Every player gets 5 secret tiles (one of each color) arranged from lowest to highest. You can see all opponents' numbers, while your own numbers stay hidden.

On Your Turn:

  1. Draw 1 Tile: Click the draw pile to reveal 1 new tile and add it to the face-up clue pool.
  2. Select 1 Available Clue Tile: Click any face-up tile in the clue pool and choose an opponent to answer one of two clues:
    • SORT: The opponent places that clue tile into its exact ascending spot among your 5 secret tiles (before #1, between two tiles, or after #5).
    • COMPARE: Pick 1 of your 5 secret slots. The opponent tells you YES (placed straight) or NO (placed angled) if the clue tile has the exact same number of dots as your secret tile.

GOT FIVE! Guess: At any point during your turn, click GOT FIVE! and submit your 5 numbers in ascending order. If all 5 are right, you win! If even one is wrong, you simply hear "Wrong guess! Try again." and play continues.
